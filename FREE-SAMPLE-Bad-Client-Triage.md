## The core math

**Effective hourly rate = Fee ÷ (Hours worked + Revision hours + Admin/chasing hours)**

Unpaid labor is real labor. It counts.

**Pain score (0–100) = 40% revision load + 30% late-payment load + 30% admin/chasing load**

**Verdicts (v2.0):** FIRE if effective rate is below your walk-away minimum,
pain ≥ 70, or 3+ red flags. FIX if below your target rate (but above
walk-away), pain 45–69, or 2 red flags. Otherwise KEEP.
