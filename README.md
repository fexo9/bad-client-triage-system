# Bad-Client Triage & Firing System — v2.0

**$29 one-time. Commercial license.**

Some clients don't pay you — they cost you. This system replaces the fear
of firing with arithmetic.

An Excel scorecard that computes the truth per client: your **EFFECTIVE
hourly rate** (fee ÷ every hour — worked, revised, chased), a 0–100 pain
score, and an automatic verdict: **FIRE, FIX, or KEEP**. Then it answers
"can I afford to fire them?" with pipeline math.

## What it is

- **Client-Profitability-Scorecard.xlsx** — 5 sheets: Start Here,
  Register (20 slots, 5 sample clients), Scorecard (automatic verdicts),
  Affordability (pipeline math + exit verdict), Worked Example (live
  fictional triage: 1 FIRE, 1 FIX, 1 KEEP).
- **Quick-start guide** — the 15-minute first run, the math, the FIX
  conversation, the exit, the 30-day re-score, common mistakes.
- **Worked example** — complete fictional triage (Meridian Design Co),
  every number shown.
- **Red-flag inventory** — the 4 firing signals with behavioral examples.
- **Decision matrix** — verdict meanings, FIX checklist, 30-day re-score,
  firing priority.
- **Exit scripts + handover** — professional termination templates (not
  legal advice), handover checklist, competitor-referral wording.
- **FAQ + limits** — 12 product-specific FAQs + honest limits.

- Does NOT guarantee firing the right clients. It calculates from YOUR
  numbers.
- Does NOT predict the future. The pipeline math uses YOUR close-rate
  estimate.
- Does NOT give legal advice. Exit scripts are professional templates.
- Does NOT fire on fee size alone — verdicts weigh economics + behavior.
- Does NOT guarantee profit, sales, or any business outcome.
